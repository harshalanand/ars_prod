## Listing Part 2 pool pre-filter (commit 88567ef, FS-LPP-01..03)
- **What:** Part 2 used to fan every MSA (MAJ_CAT, GEN_ART, CLR) across all active stores as `IS_NEW=1`, whatever the run pool held. `_msa_pool_filter` now keeps only options with pool FNL_Q>0 OR a live prior-run hold, and applies to both the diagnostic and the INSERT.
- **Why:** session 20261009_161826_077 hung 5h 06m in CXSYNC_PORT over 38.8M rows. MSA distinct options had grown 21,948 → 103,654, almost all with zero FRESH-pool stock.
- **Invariant:** the gate is a strict subset of Part 6.6 `gate_stock` and Stage A `R04`, which stay in place. The hold lookup is an EXISTS that mirrors Part 3.54, with table-qualified outer columns and a TRY_CAST for the nvarchar-vs-bigint GEN_ART key.
- **Measured:** -96.7% (101,383 → 3,343) on 10-10 and -13.9% (14,277 → 12,295) on 10-12. The percentage tracks that day's dead stock, so the invariant (0 dropped options with a live hold) is the thing to check. It held on both dates.
- **Loose ends (not fixed):** the manual bullet cites a `BRD/FSD_LISTING_POOL_PREFILTER.md` that does not exist; `verify_pool_prefilter.py` copies the build_filter construction and nothing enforces parity with the endpoint.
