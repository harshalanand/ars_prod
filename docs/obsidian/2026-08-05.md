## Allocation "Cap Ladder" — 3-Round Waterfall with Family Borrowing (SPEC APPROVED, Implementation Deferred)

See [[project_allocation_cap_ladder]] for full design document.

**Status:** Spec finalized and approved by Santosh 2026-08-05 ("perfect"). Implementation NOT started. Design conversation used live probes on rep_data.

### The Three Rounds

#### Round 1: "BASE" @100% Flat

- **Walk:** Normal (RL→TBC→TBL, store rank)
- **Walls checked:** EVERY wall at 100% — MJ, tier grids, merge grids (merge IS used in Round 1, validator role), merge2 if present
- **MJ_REQ:** At 100%
- **Leftover:** Remembered for Round 2

#### Round 2: "GROWTH" @Growth-Matrix %

- **Scope:** Leftovers only
- **Wallets:** Own wallets grown
- **Borrowing:** None
- **Matrix:** Per-OPT_TYPE sliders rl_mbq_cap_pct / tbc_mbq_cap_pct / tbl_mbq_cap_pct (default 130 when unset; matrix chosen over fixed 130)
- **MJ_REQ:** Recomputed at matrix %

#### Round 3: "BORROW"

- **Scope:** Leftovers only
- **Per blocked wall:** Own wall is LIFTED → family wallet's REMAINDER @ matrix % becomes the only ceiling
- **Merge2+:** Still validated
- **Walls without family:** Stay hard (no borrow)
- **Tags:** LADDER_OVERFLOW[family] / LADDER_EXHAUSTED[family]

### Invariants (Hard Rules)

1. **Own wallet first:** Every wall borrows ONLY from its own family.
2. **Family total never exceeded:** Every shipped piece deducts from EVERY wallet it belongs to (tier+merge+merge2+MJ).
3. **All walls must still pass:** AND across dimensions (per blocking wall chain).
4. **Negative families never lend:** Over-stocked families stay blocked (protected).
5. **Unfed walls escalate:** MBQ=0 walls escalate to family. If no fed family exists, stay blocked (e.g., junk 'NA'/'1' stays blocked).
6. **MJ is absolute ceiling:** Never borrows.
7. **No duplicate rows:** Rounds top up same allocation line; reuses TBL hold-retry re-injection pattern in rule_engine_pandas.py.

### Family Chain Resolution

Family resolved from naming: FAB → MERGE_FAB → MERGE2_FAB (via new merge_no helpers). Dimensions opt in by having merge rules. **Think-twice:** vendor/colour pooling flagged as business decision (can be family).

- **HB10 / M_TEES_HS** — E tier −289 vs EV family +3,104 (classic borrow case: E allocs from EV remainder)
- **HO23 / PSP family** — negative −1,298 (protection case: PSP doesn't lend despite own wall block)

### Why (Business Rationale)

If individual RNG_SEG not fed → alloc based on merge, then validate merge2. **Sequence:**

1. Fill RNG_SEG first (ROUND 1)
2. Then merge (ROUND 2–ROUND 3)
3. Then merge2 (ROUND 3)

During merge alloc, individual cap removed. Example: E filled to cap, V has no stock → E allocates up to X% of merge. **Bonus:** Round 1 serves every store's base need before any store's growth (fairness by construction).

### Rollout Strategy

**Business rule:** `ALC_CAP_LADDER` = OFF | SHADOW | ON (default OFF).

1. Deploy OFF → byte-identical OFF-regression
2. SHADOW 3–5 production runs → real dispatch today, ladder outcome computed + tagged for "would-have-shipped" report
3. Review borrowed-quantity report
4. ON (instant rollback: set to OFF)

### Worked Example (Wallet Model)

Suppose stores request (RL/TBC/TBL/total): E 40/0/0, V 0/80/0, P 0/0/30, SP 0/0/20.

**Wallets available (own / family):**
- E: 100 / EV family 500
- V: 400 / EV family 500
- P: 450 / PSP family 500
- SP: 50 / PSP family 500
- EV merge family: 500
- PSP merge family: 500

**Round 1 (BASE @100%):**
- E requests 40 vs cap 100 → ships 40 ✓
- V requests 80 vs cap 400 → ships 80 ✓
- P requests 30 vs cap 450 → ships 30 ✓
- SP requests 20 vs cap 50 → ships 20 ✓
- **Leftovers:** None (all met at base)

**Round 2 (GROWTH @ matrix % e.g., 130%):**
- No leftovers, skip

**Round 3 (BORROW):**
- No leftovers, skip

**Outcome:** All 170 pcs shipped. Family wallets all partial (E used 40≤250 in EV, V used 80≤250 in EV; P used 30≤250 in PSP, SP used 20≤250 in PSP).
